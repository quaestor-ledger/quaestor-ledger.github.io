Multi-tenant AR/AP ledger

Observe, record, and prove.

Quaestor records what is owed and what was paid elsewhere. It gives teams an auditable ledger and operational evidence without initiating transfers, holding funds, or becoming a payment rail.

Explore the repositories Review the ledger server

Operating model

A ledger with a deliberately narrow boundary

Observe

Ingest provider and application events through explicit, tenant-scoped interfaces.

Record

Preserve double-entry facts, membership decisions, and audit evidence in the system of record.

Prove

Expose reproducible balances, invariants, and tamper-evident verification without taking custody.

Architecture

Identity and authorization stay separate

1 Shared Auth

Establishes identity, active session state, and assurance.

2 Quaestor grants

Authorize exact tenants and billing scopes in Quaestor Postgres.

3 Ledger operations

Apply tenant-local policy and preserve reviewable evidence.

A valid identity token never grants access to a tenant by itself.

Quaestor observes, records, and proves. It never moves or holds money.
